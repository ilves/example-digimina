# Core question bank

Pick, don't recite. Skip what you already know. Follow up on anything interesting with "say more about that".

- `[options: …]` means: offer these as clickable choices if your tool has a question/options tool, otherwise list them inline. Always allow the owner's own answer.
- **(quick)** marks the questions used in the quick start.

## Sources (→ me.md, Where my data lives)

- Which of these hold your data, and should I read them when they're connected? `[options, multi-select: Calendar · Email · Task board (Notion, Todoist…) · Investments (e.g. Portfellow) · Training or health app (e.g. Strava) · None]`
- Where should tasks and numbers live? `[options: In this repo · In those tools, the repo keeps the context · A mix (tell me which)]`

## Round 1 — A normal week

- Walk me through a normal weekday, from waking up to going to bed. Rough is fine.
- And a normal weekend?
- Who are the people you spend the most time with? (Names or roles, whichever you prefer.)
- What part of your week do you look forward to most?
- What part of your week would you happily never do again?
- What's taking up more of your head than it should right now?

## Round 2 — How to talk to me (→ me.md)

Snapshot:
- What should I call you? **(quick)**
- Roughly what age or life stage are you in? (A decade is enough.) `[options: 20s · 30s · 40s · 50s · 60s+]`
- Where do you live? City or region is enough.
- What do you do for work, in one line?
- Who's in your household?

How to talk to you:
- Which language should I answer in? **(quick)** `[options: the language they're writing in · English · Other]`
- Short and direct, or with more explanation? **(quick)** `[options: Short and direct · More explanation · Depends on the topic]`
- When should I push back on you? **(quick)**
- Is there anything AI assistants do that annoys you? (That goes under "Never".) **(quick)** `[options, multi-select: Long disclaimers · Flattery · Too many questions · Long answers · Other]`

How you decide:
- Think of a recent decision you're happy with. How did you make it?
- And one you regret. What went wrong in how you decided?
- Do you tend to overthink, or to rush? `[options: Overthink · Rush · Depends on the size of the decision]`

Blind spots (ask the first; the second is a follow-up if the first doesn't land):
- What would a close friend say you tend to get wrong?
- When do you usually realise too late that you should have stopped?

## Round 3 — What's on the plate (→ now.md)

- If this month went really well, what three things would have moved?
- What have you started but not finished?
- What are you waiting on from someone else?
- What dates matter in the next 30 days? Trips, deadlines, events, payments.
- Anything else on your mind that a good assistant should know about this month?

## Round 4 — Choosing areas

- Which of these should we set up? `[options, multi-select: your 2–4 suggested areas · Something else]`
- Which one first? `[options: the chosen areas]`
- Is there a decision coming up in the next few months that you'd like help with?
