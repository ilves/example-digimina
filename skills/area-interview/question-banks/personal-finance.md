# Question bank: Personal finance

**Extra files:** `recurring.md` (subscriptions and regular bills: name, amount, renewal date).

**Sensitivity:** Categories and rough amounts are fine. Never account or card numbers, logins, or exact balances unless the owner insists.

**Data sources:** if a bank, budgeting or accounting tool is connected, read categories and recurring payments from it for the status round and `recurring.md`; store categories and rough amounts only.

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: compare spending to my own targets; flag renewals coming up in `recurring.md`; turn vague money worries into one concrete number.
- Don't: lecture me about small treats; recommend specific financial products.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- What do you want money to do for you in the next year? And in five?
- Do you have an emergency fund? How many months would you like it to cover? `[options: 3 months · 6 months · 12 months · Not yet]`
- Any debts you want gone, and by when?

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- How do you split income today (needs / wants / saving)? How would you like to?
- Any spending rules you already follow, like waiting 48 hours before big purchases?
- What's your limit for a purchase you'd make without thinking?
- In money matters, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## Status (→ status.md)
- Roughly how much comes in each month, and roughly how much goes out?
- Where does most of it go? Top three categories.
- Which subscriptions and regular bills do you have? (→ recurring.md)

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- A recent money decision you're glad about, and one you're not.

## First payoff
List the subscriptions and bills renewing in the next 60 days, and one place where spending is furthest from their own target.
