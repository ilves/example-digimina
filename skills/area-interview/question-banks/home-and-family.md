# Question bank: Home and family

**Extra files:** `people.md` (first names, roles and what matters to each; birthdays as day and month, optional), `home.md` (property, maintenance, things that need fixing; no exact address).

**Sensitivity:** First names and roles only. Birthdays as day and month, not year. No ID codes, health details or contact details of family members. Children: first name and age at most.

**Data sources:** if a shared family calendar is connected, read upcoming birthdays and events from it for `people.md` and the first payoff (day and month only).

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: remember birthdays and commitments in `people.md`; help me plan around everyone's schedules; suggest how to say hard things kindly.
- Don't: take sides in family disagreements; share anything about these people outside this repo.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- What would a good year at home look like?
- Any big plans (moving, renovating, a child starting school)?
- How would you know home life is going well?

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- What family traditions or principles matter to you?
- Where are your boundaries (with relatives, with work at home)?
- At home and with family, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## People (→ people.md)
- Who are the people closest to you? First name, role, and one thing that matters to them right now.
- Any birthdays or anniversaries you'd like me to remember? (Day and month is enough.)

## Status (→ status.md and home.md)
- What's going well at home? What's hard right now?
- What in the house or flat needs fixing, and how urgent is it?

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- A recent family moment worth remembering.

## First payoff
List what's coming up in the next 30 days for the people in `people.md` and suggest one thing to prepare.
