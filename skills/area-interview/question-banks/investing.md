# Question bank: Investing

**Extra files:** `watchlist.md`, `theses/` (one file per position, from `theses/_thesis-template.md`). The investing area in the template already contains them.

**Sensitivity:** Weights in %, not account balances or holding values, unless the owner insists. A regular contribution ("€500 a month") is fine to write down. Never account numbers, logins or ID codes. Say this before the status round.

**Data sources:** if a portfolio tracker (for example Portfellow) or a broker is connected, read holdings, weights, contributions, returns and the worst drawdown from it before the status round. Show what you found and ask only to confirm. Store weights in %, as above.

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: check every idea against `rules.md` and say clearly when a rule is broken; play devil's advocate on theses; point out patterns in the journal (buying after rises, selling after drops); say when you don't know a number and send them to the source.
- Don't: give buy or sell recommendations; agree just because the owner sounds sure.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- What is this money for? What would it let you do?
- When will you start needing it? `[options: Within 5 years · 5–10 years · 10–20 years · 20+ years]` Follow-up: is any of it needed sooner?
- How much do you put in each month or year, and how steady is that?
- Which accounts or wrappers do you use? Names only, no numbers. `[options, multi-select: Investment account · Pension fund · Company · Other]`
- How would you know you're on track? A savings rate, a milestone, a percentage of a target?

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- If you had to write your strategy in three sentences, what would they be?
- What's your target split (for example funds vs. single stocks vs. cash)? What range around it is OK?
- Is there a maximum size for one position? `[options: 5% · 8% · 10% · No limit yet]`
- When do you rebalance? `[options: Once a year · When a bucket drifts off target · Never so far]`
- When do you sell? Not for a specific stock, in general.
- What do you never buy?
- **Behaviour, not intentions:** the last time markets fell hard, what did you actually do? `[options: Held · Bought more · Sold some · Sold a lot · Wasn't investing yet]` Follow-up: how did it feel?
- In investing, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## Status (→ status.md)
- What do you hold right now and roughly what weight is each? Approximate is fine; a screenshot with numbers hidden works too.
- Is anything currently outside your own rules?
- Anything planned or changing soon (a big contribution, a withdrawal, a pause)?

After status, check the holdings against the rules and fill the **Rule check** section. Mention any breach plainly, without telling them what to do.

## Theses (→ theses/<name>.md)
For the largest one to three single positions (skip broad index funds unless they want one):
- Why do you own it, in two sentences?
- What has to happen for it to work out?
- What would prove you wrong?
- When would you sell?
- What worries you about it?

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- What was your last buy or sell, and why?
- Is there a trade you regret? What were you feeling at the time?

## First payoff
At Close, show the **Rule check** from `status.md` in three lines (you already wrote it in the status section; don't run a second one). Then offer the devil's advocate prompt (`prompts/devils-advocate.md`) on their largest single position.
