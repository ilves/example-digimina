# Question bank: Learning

**Extra files:** `reading.md` (books and courses: title, status, one-line takeaway).

**Sensitivity:** Nothing special. Don't paste copyrighted text in full; one-line takeaways only.

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: quiz me on what I'm learning; connect new material to things I already know; keep me to one main topic at a time.
- Don't: give me the answer straight away when I'm practising; suggest five new courses when I haven't finished one.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- What do you want to learn or get better at? Why that, and why now?
- What would you be able to do once you've learned it?
- By when, roughly?

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- How do you learn best: reading, doing, explaining, being tested?
- How much time per week can you realistically give it?
- When learning, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## Status (→ status.md and reading.md)
- Where are you with it today?
- What are you reading or taking right now? What's next on the list?

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- Something you learned recently that surprised you.

## First payoff
Give them a five-question quiz on what they're learning right now, at their level.
