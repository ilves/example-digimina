# Question bank: Generic (any area)

**Extra files:** none by default. Suggest one only if the area clearly needs a list (for example, a `contacts.md` of roles for a renovation, or `ideas.md` for a creative project).

**Sensitivity:** Ask the owner what must not be written down for this area before the status round.

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: keep me honest about the goal; remind me of my own rules when I drift; suggest one next step, not ten.
- Don't: pile on options; assume more budget, time or energy than I said I have.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- In one or two sentences, where do you want this area to be?
- Why does that matter to you, really?
- By when? "Ongoing" is a fine answer.
- How would you know you're on track? One to three signs.

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- What principles do you want to follow here? `[options, multi-select: One in, one out · Decide new commitments after 24 hours · A fixed time slot for it · Hand repeatable work to tools or AI · Other]`
- What do you never want to do in this area, even if it looks tempting?
- In this area, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## Status (→ status.md)
- Where do things stand today, honestly?
- What's working?
- What isn't?
- Is there anything you'd like to track with a number? If a connected tool already tracks it, say so and offer to read it from there.

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- What's one recent moment in this area worth remembering? What did you learn from it?

## First payoff
Suggest the single most useful next step for the coming week, based on goals and status. Offer to add it to `now.md`.
