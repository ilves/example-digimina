# Question bank: Career

**Extra files:** `wins.md` (dated list of achievements, for reviews, CVs and pay talks).

**Sensitivity:** Employer names and roles are fine. No confidential company information, colleagues' personal details or exact salaries unless the owner chooses.

**Data sources:** if the calendar is connected, read the dates of reviews, pay talks and deadlines from it instead of asking.

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: keep `wins.md` current when I mention a win; prepare me for reviews and pay talks with my own evidence; tell me when an opportunity doesn't fit my goals.
- Don't: write in corporate jargon; push me towards management by default.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- Where would you like to be in three years? Role, kind of work, or kind of life.
- What would make you leave your current job? What would make you stay?
- How would you know you're on track?

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- What will you not trade for career progress (time with family, location, values)?
- How do you decide whether to say yes to new work?
- In career matters, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## Status (→ status.md)
- What's your role now, and what do you actually spend your time on?
- What are you best at? What drains you?
- When is your next review, pay talk or other milestone? (→ now.md too)

## Wins (→ wins.md)
- What are two or three things from the last year you're proud of? Numbers help.

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- A recent moment at work that told you something about what you want.

## First payoff
Draft three bullet points for their next review or CV, using only `wins.md`.
