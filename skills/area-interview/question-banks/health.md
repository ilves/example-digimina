# Question bank: Health

**Extra files:** `routines.md` (training, sleep and food routines as they actually are).

**Sensitivity:** Habits, goals and how the owner feels are fine. Diagnoses, medications and test results only if the owner explicitly wants them written down. Never other people's health details.

**Data sources:** if a training or health app (for example Strava, Garmin, Apple Health via a connector) is connected, read recent activity, records and sleep from it before the status round and confirm instead of asking.

**Default AI rules to offer:**
- Do: plan around my real schedule and energy; notice when I'm overdoing it; keep advice general and send me to a professional for anything medical.
- Don't: give calorie targets or weight-loss plans unless I ask; shame me for missed days.

## Goals (→ goals.md)
- What does "healthy" look like for you a year from now? What would you be able to do? `[options, multi-select: Fitter or faster (a race or test goal) · Stronger · More energy and better sleep · Long-term health and staying injury-free · Other]`
- Why now? `[options, multi-select: Age · Family · Momentum I don't want to lose · Energy for work and projects · Other]`
- How would you know you're getting there? `[options, multi-select: A race or test time · Sessions per week · Strength markers · Sleep hours · Other]`

## Rules (→ rules.md)
- What principles do you want to keep? `[options, multi-select: Easy days stay easy · Never make up a missed session the next day · Every 4th week lighter · A minimum dose in busy weeks · No screens after 22:00 · Other]`
- What should you never do, given your body and history? Only as much detail as you're comfortable with.
- In health, what do you want me to do, and what should I never do?

## Status (→ status.md)
- How are you sleeping, moving and eating these days, honestly?
- What's working? What keeps slipping?
- Anything you'd like to track (steps, runs per week, hours of sleep)?

## Journal seed (→ journal.md)
- A recent week that went well for your health. What made it work?

## First payoff
Propose a realistic plan for the next seven days that fits the week in `me.md` and the dates in `now.md`.
