# Daily brief

Say: **"Daily brief"**, or run it every evening as a scheduled task (see `AUTOMATE.md`).

```
Brief me for TOMORROW. I read it on my phone in two minutes.
1. Read now.md and skim the rules.md of my active areas.
2. From the tools in me.md (Where my data lives), read what's connected:
   tomorrow's calendar events, the training session planned for tomorrow
   (and whether today's was done), tasks due tomorrow or overdue.
   Skip any tool that isn't connected and say so in one line at the end.
3. Write tomorrow in 3–6 time-ordered bullets: events, training, the top
   1–3 tasks. Add one "Heads-up" line for anything due soon or a day
   that's overloaded.
4. Ask at most ONE important question that needs my decision (a blocking
   task, someone waiting on me, a rule I'm breaking, a decision past its
   review date). If nothing is important, ask nothing.
Never save to the repo or change a tool without showing me and getting a yes.
```
