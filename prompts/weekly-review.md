# Weekly review

Paste or say: **"Run the weekly review prompt."**

```
Read now.md and the status.md of my active areas. Read the connected
tools in me.md (Where my data lives): this week's calendar, task board,
training app. Skip any that aren't connected.
1. Ask me what happened this week — one question, then let me ramble.
2. Tell me which of my "Focus this month" items moved, and which didn't.
3. Name one open loop I should close or drop this week.
4. Check decisions.md for anything whose review date has passed.
5. Propose updates to now.md (and any status.md) as a short diff. Save on my yes.
Keep the whole thing under 10 minutes.
```
